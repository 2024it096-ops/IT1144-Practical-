Course Details

DIPLOMA IN ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY (DET)

Duration: 1 Year

Description: Covers core concepts in electrical, mechanical, and civil engineering with hands-on training and industry-based modules.

Prerequisites: G.C.E. A/L in Science or Technology stream

DIPLOMA IN INFORMATION & COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY (DICT)

Duration: 1 Year

Description: Provides practical knowledge in IT fundamentals, software development, databases, and networking technologies.

Prerequisites: G.C.E. O/L with basic computer literacy

BACHELOR OF ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY HONOURS (BET HONS)

Duration: 4 Years

Description: An advanced degree in engineering with specialization in areas such as electronics, telecommunications, and civil engineering design.

Prerequisites: A relevant diploma or G.C.E. A/L with required results

BACHELOR IN INFORMATION & COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY (BICT HONS)

Duration: 4 Years

Description: Focuses on software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and enterprise-level IT systems development.

Prerequisites: DICT or A/L in Maths/Science stream
